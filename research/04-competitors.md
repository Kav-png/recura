## The gap (synthesis)
No incumbent is voice-agent-first + consumer-wearable BYOD + hospital-clinicians-in-loop + priced self-funding for mid-size penalized hospitals & at-risk physician groups. **No vendor found marketing billing workflows built around 99445/99470 2–15-day episodes (codes live only since Jan 2026) → first-mover window.**
Credible fast-follow threat: Commure (Memora) or Cadence adding voice + episodic codes; Epic adding post-discharge engagement.
