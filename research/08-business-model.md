# Business Model — The Money Loop (canonical statement)

## One sentence
We sell REVENUE, not software: an AI care layer letting physician groups deliver and bill the post-discharge care Medicare already pays for (~$274–372/episode, uncaptured on ~82% of eligible discharges), with readmission & mortality reduction riding on the same billing codes.

## The loop
1. Discharge letter parsed → patient enrolled (bedside, <10 min, by TCM-billing practice staff).
2. AI does the volume work: daily voice check-ins, wearable ingestion, symptom capture, triage, documentation drafting, scheduling the F2F.
3. Human clinician performs the billable moments (TCM 2-day contact; RPM/RTM live interactive touch) — legally required (see 03-reimbursement-codes.md ⚠️) and the trust posture ("nurse-supervised AI").
4. Practice bills TCM (99495/96) + RPM (99445/99470 or 99454/99457) → ~$274–372 new revenue/episode.
5. We charge per episode (~$75–100 working hypothesis) → product is SELF-FUNDING for the buyer; no ROI-committee war.
6. Detection layer accumulates outcome-labeled data (symptoms+vitals+adherence × readmitted-or-not × clinician confirm/dismiss) → precision moat competitors can't shortcut (Jay's 16-minute-clone test answered).

## Buyer sequence
1. At-risk physician enterprises / ACOs / MSSP / MA — bill the codes AND keep savings; fastest cycle.
2. Mid-size hospitals with ≥1% HRRP penalties (public CMS list = target list) — after value-based proof; FY2027 MA inclusion expands this pool sharply.
Avoid first: FFS hospital CFO cost-avoidance sales (12–18 mo, ~9 stakeholders).

## Clinical scope discipline
Beachhead HF + COPD (strong positive evidence); avoid undifferentiated sepsis/complex-elderly (UPMC harm signal); graded respond-at-home protocols; alert PRECISION as first-class KPI.

## Kill criteria (from strategy memo §10)
Reimbursement counsel invalidates human-in-loop billing design; alert precision won't beat fatigue thresholds; enrollment <40% of offered patients; pilot >9 months to sign → pivot buyer.
